# Cofounder Agreement

This Cofounder Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [Effective Date] between [Founder A] and [Founder B] (each a "Founder" and together the "Founders") in connection with [Company Name], a [State] [entity type] (the "Company").

## Purpose

The Founders are starting the Company together. This Agreement records how they will share ownership, divide responsibilities, make decisions and handle a Founder leaving, so that each Founder knows what to expect from the other.

## Roles and Commitment

[Founder A] will serve as [Title] and is responsible for [areas of responsibility]. [Founder B] will serve as [Title] and is responsible for [areas of responsibility].

Each Founder will devote [full time / a stated number of hours per week] to the Company. A Founder who is not yet full time will become full time by [date or milestone].

## Equity

The Company's founder shares are divided as follows: [Founder A], [percentage]%; [Founder B], [percentage]%.

Each Founder's shares are subject to vesting as described under Vesting.

## Vesting

Each Founder's shares vest over [four] years from [vesting start date], with [25]% vesting on the first anniversary (the "Cliff") and the rest vesting in equal monthly installments after that, as long as the Founder continues to provide services to the Company.

If the Company is acquired, [no acceleration / single-trigger acceleration / double-trigger acceleration] applies to unvested shares.

## Intellectual Property

Each Founder assigns to the Company all inventions, code, designs and other work product they create for the Company's business, including work created before the Effective Date that is listed in Schedule A.

Anything a Founder created before the Effective Date and does not list in Schedule A remains that Founder's own property.

## Decision-Making

Decisions in a Founder's area of responsibility are made by that Founder. The following decisions require the agreement of both Founders: [raising money, hiring and firing, taking on debt, selling the Company, changing this Agreement].

If the Founders cannot agree on a decision that requires both of them, they will [discuss it in good faith for at least [number] days, then use the deadlock process they have chosen].

## Compensation and Expenses

Until the Company raises outside funding, [neither Founder takes a salary / each Founder is paid as follows]. The Company reimburses reasonable business expenses a Founder pays with prior approval from the other Founder.

## Departure of a Founder

If a Founder stops providing services to the Company for any reason, their unvested shares are subject to repurchase by the Company at the lower of the original purchase price and fair market value.

A departing Founder keeps their vested shares. [The Company has a right of first refusal on any transfer of vested shares.]

## Confidentiality

Each Founder will keep the Company's confidential information private, during and after their time with the Company, and use it only for the Company's business.

## Non-Solicitation

For [twelve] months after leaving the Company, a departing Founder will not solicit the Company's employees or customers away from the Company, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## Dispute Resolution

The Founders will first try to resolve any dispute under this Agreement by discussing it directly. If that fails, they will try mediation before starting any other proceeding.

## General Terms

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [State]. It can be changed only in writing signed by both Founders. If any part of this Agreement is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.

This Agreement is the Founders' understanding of their relationship and is intended to be reflected in the Company's formation documents, including each Founder's stock purchase agreement.
